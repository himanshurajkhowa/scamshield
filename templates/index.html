      BEHIND THE SHIELD

      A second look,
      before the next click.

      Scams borrow familiar voices: a bank, a courier, sometimes a friend. ScamShield brings message classification and explainable warning signs into one simple check.

      How it works

      A lightweight TF-IDF and logistic regression pipeline classifies spam versus non-spam. Separate rules look for OTP and UPI PIN requests, KYC threats, payment pressure and unusual links. The model was trained on the UCI SMS Spam Collection. Links are parsed on the server and never opened.

      Built with India in mind

      Rules cover common Indian payment and identity-verification patterns. The model was trained on UCI’s English SMS Spam Collection; this is not an Indian-trained fraud model. English and Hinglish text can be pasted, but Hinglish accuracy is not validated.

      Know its limits

      Spam is not the same as fraud. The model can miss new scams and flag legitimate messages. It cannot verify a sender, scan a destination website or confirm whether money is safe to send. Feedback stays in this page and does not train the model.

      What could come next

      Permitted WhatsApp Business integrations, authorized email connections, a browser extension, an agent API or skill, consented notifications, verified feedback with supervised retraining, and more Indian languages. Offline and edge inference are future possibilities, not tested features.

      Stay in control

      Open your bank or courier’s official app independently. Never share a secret code or PIN with another person.
